🍽️ Replate – AI-Based Food Waste Redistribution System

Replate is a technology-driven platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting surplus food providers with people and organizations that can redistribute it to those in need.

The project focuses on making food donation and redistribution **faster, smarter, and more organized** using digital tools and AI-based assistance.



🌟 Project Objective

Every day, a large amount of edible food is wasted while many people struggle to access proper meals.

Replate aims to bridge this gap** by creating a platform where surplus food can be listed, discovered, collected, and redistributed efficiently.



🚀 Key Features

* 🍱 Surplus food listing and donation
* 📍 Location-based food discovery
* 🤝 Connection between donors and receiving organizations
* 🧠 AI-assisted food redistribution and recommendations
* 📊 Organized tracking of available food donations
* 🔔 Notifications and status updates
* 👤 User-friendly donor and receiver interface
* ♻️ Focus on reducing food waste and promoting sustainability


💡 How Replate Works

1. Food Provider uploads surplus food details
2. Replate identifies suitable recipients or organizations
3. Nearby available food can be discovered
4. Pickup or redistribution is coordinated
5. Donation status is updated after completion
